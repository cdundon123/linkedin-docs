# About LinkedIn Premium Career

## Overview

LinkedIn Premium Career is a paid subscription service that enhances the free LinkedIn experience with advanced tools for job seekers, sales professionals, and business growth. Key features include InMail messages to contact anyone, insight into who viewed your profile, unlimited browsing, and AI-powered learning resources.

[Start a free trial of LinkedIn Premium Career](https://premium.linkedin.com/careers/career) to use the features included in this guide.
