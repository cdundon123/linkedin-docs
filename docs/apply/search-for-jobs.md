# Search for jobs

## Overview

LinkedIn allows you to strategically search for jobs according to title, required skillsets, pay, location, company type, and more. You can use its search and explore features to find jobs that align with your [job preferences](../apply/add-job-preferences.md). 

## Search by job title, skill, or company

1. Click on the **Jobs** icon in the top navigation bar. 

    ![job icon](../images/jobs-icon.png)

1. Enter your desired job title, skillset, or company name into the search bar. You can also specify a location (city, state, or country) to refine your search.

    ![job search](../images/job-search.png)

1. Scan search results for jobs that interest you.

1. *(Optional)* Click **Save** to save a job listing for later. You can access saved jobs by returning to the Jobs landing page and clicking **Job tracker**.

## Explore with job collections

LinkedIn also allows you to explore jobs by pre-curated job collections, such as Unicorns, Easy Apply, and Series AB (see [Reference: Job collections](../references/job-collections.md) to learn more about individual job collection categories).

1. From the Jobs landing page, scroll down to reach the **Explore with job collections** section. 
1. Click **Show all**.
1. Select a job collection to filter job listings.
